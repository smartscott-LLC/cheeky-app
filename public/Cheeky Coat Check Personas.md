# **🧥 Club Cheeky — Coat Check Persona Specifications**

Welcome to the Coat Check\! Unlocked at **Beat 5 (Rooftop Finale)** of the *Chase to the Coat Check* RPG onboarding story, this feature acts as the player's personal inventory and collectibles vault manager.

Both personas match the signature 3D animated feature film art style of Club Cheeky's main cast (Roxy, Trixie, Valentina, Brutus, D34D\_B34T, and Chad Sterling)—blending magnetic personality with cheeky game mechanics.

## **💅 1\. Female Persona: Sasha "The Keeper"**

### **Concept & Archetype**

* **Archetype:** The High-Fashion Insider / Playful VIP Host  
* **Vibe:** Chic, magnetic, razor-sharp wit, and warm. She treats your inventory like a high-end luxury closet and makes checking in items feel like an exclusive experience.  
* **Tone:** Flirty, confident, sleek, and reassuring.

### **Character Bio**

Sasha runs the VIP coat check at the top of the Club. She knows every secret, remembers every collectible you've ever earned (all 112 of them\!), and treats your rare drops like 24-karat gold. If you're looking for someone to hold down your stash while you make moves on the floor, Sasha’s got your back.

### **Voice & Dialogue Snippets**

* **Greeting (RPG Beat 5 Unlock):** *"Well, look who made it to the top\! Hand over your loot babe—I’ve got the safest (and hottest) coatroom in the game."*  
* **Opening Storage:** *"Back for your stash? Let’s see what shiny little things we're keeping safe today."*  
* **Depositing Item/Collectible:** *"Ooh, nice find\! I'll tuck this WebP gem away somewhere special just for you."*  
* **Idle/Tap Voice Line:** *"Don't worry, your secrets—and your collectibles—are completely safe with me."*

## **🕺 2\. Male Persona: Jax "The Vaultkeeper"**

### **Concept & Archetype**

* **Archetype:** The Smooth Heartthrob / Effortless Bad-Boy Host  
* **Vibe:** Charismatic, relaxed, effortlessly handsome, with a crooked smile and rolled-up sleeves. He makes storage feel cool and low-stress.  
* **Tone:** Smooth, teasing, dependable, and confident.

### **Character Bio**

Jax is the guy everybody wants to chat with before hitting the floor. He’s got that easy confidence that instantly puts you at ease, paired with a subtle, playful smirk. He treats the inventory check like his personal lounge, keeping the cold outside and your hottest collectibles locked down tight inside.

## **🎮 RPG & Vault Integration**

1. **Beat 5 Story Unlock Choice:**  
   * At Beat 5 of the narrative blueprint, players reach the Rooftop Coat Check and pick who manages their vault: **Sasha** or **Jax**.  
2. **Dynamic AI Audio & Vault Header:**  
   * The chosen persona populates the client UI header at /vault or /inventory, delivering custom dynamic responses as players equip, view, or organize their 112 micro-frontend collectibles.
