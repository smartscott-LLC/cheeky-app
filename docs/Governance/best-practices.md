# Club Cheeky — Best Practices & External-Activity Disclaimer

> Binding policy. Mirrored on the in-app `/best-practices` page. Version `v1`.
> Signed at signup as the `best_practices` consent.

## Why this exists

Club Cheeky is a **place you enter and leave**. The club runs inside the app —
events, chats, matches, gift dates — and that is the environment we can keep
safe, moderated, and governed. What members choose to do **outside** the app
is their choice, and their responsibility.

## Best practices (in-app)

1. **Stay in the club.** Keep the conversation inside Club Cheeky. If someone
   pushes you to move to another app, phone, or messaging platform early, that
   is a signal — not a red flag by itself, but slow down and trust your gut.
2. **The badge is real, the future is not guaranteed.** Verification proves a
   real ID cleared Brutus. It does not guarantee character. Take your time;
   the club rewards patience, never pressure.
3. **Never send money.** Anyone asking for money, gift cards, crypto, or
   "help with an emergency" is a scammer. Report and block them — Brutus will
   bounce them.
4. **Personal info is earned, not owed.** Share what you want, when you want.
   Nobody is entitled to your address, workplace, or full name.
5. **No means no, and silence is a no.** A decline is final; no follow-ups,
   no re-approaches. That is the rule of the floor.
6. **Trust the tools.** Report and block are one tap away, from any chat.
   Moderators review every report; appeals are decided case by case.

## Meeting in person (external activity)

If you choose to meet someone in person, exchange physical phone numbers, or
interact with anyone **outside** the app, you do so **at your own risk**:

- Meet in a public place. Tell a friend where you're going.
- Arrange your own transportation. Keep your own exit plan.
- Stay sober enough to stay in control.
- Check in with someone you trust before and after.

**Club Cheeky is not responsible for anything that happens outside of the
app.** We provide the venue and the tools; we cannot police the real world.
Meet smart, take care of yourself, and know that your safety outside the club
is in your own hands — we just want you to come back.

## Enforcement

- The disclaimer is **acknowledged at signup** (the `best_practices` consent,
  version `v1`, stored in `consents`).
- This policy updates together with the code and the `/best-practices` page —
  a change to one is a change to all.
