# The Rules of the Club — Terms of Service

> **Living document — refined as the club grows.**

## 1. Getting in

- You must be **18 or older**. The club checks. No exceptions.
- One account per person. One identity per account. Fake profiles are a
  bouncing offense.
- Entry requires passing the Door Check (see `verification-age.md`) for events
  and the full club; Guests may look around the street level without one.

## 2. Membership

- **Silver card** is free: verified members get the card, the VIP badge, 20
  tokens, and event access.
- **Gold, Platinum, Diamond** are paid floors billed monthly through Stripe.
  Subscriptions auto-renew until canceled.
- **You can cancel anytime, in one click, from your Account page.** No phone
  calls, no retention scripts, no surprise charges.

## 3. Events & the Dance Floor

- Events cost tokens per floor (3 / 5 / 25 / 40…). Entry is non-refundable
  once the event runs — but see §6 for the no-match rule.
- During events: be excellent. The floor rules (§5) apply inside every song.
- Either dancer can end a song early. Both can report from inside the chat.

## 4. Tokens

- Tokens are in-app currency for events and (later) gifts. They are **not**
  redeemable for cash, not transferable between users, and not an investment.
- Earn: verification, referrals, giveaways. Buy: token packs via Stripe.
- Details in `refunds-tokens.md`.

## 5. Conduct — the floor rules

No harassment, threats, or stalking. No explicit content. No minors. No hate.
No spam or solicitation. No sharing other people's private info. No
impersonation. Violations escalate: warning → timeout → permanent bounce.

## 6. Refunds

- **No match on the Dance Floor = automatic, private token refund.** Silent
  loss, public win.
- **Event canceled for low fill = automatic refund.**
- Token pack purchases follow Stripe's refund process.
- **Monetary disputes are handled by Stripe** — we intervene only when a
  situation escalates beyond them.

## 7. Liability

- The club is provided as-is. We don't guarantee matches, responses, or
  outcomes — we guarantee a fun, fair, safe room.
- You're responsible for what you post and who you meet. Meet safely.

## 8. Changes to the rules

- Rule changes are announced before they take effect. Material changes require
  fresh consent. (Draft — final notice mechanics to be set with counsel.)
