# Keeping the Floor Safe — Community & Safety Standards (Draft)

> **Draft — legal review required before public launch.**

## The framing

The club has rules so everyone can have fun. Brutus is at the door; the floor
rules keep the room right. Safety is a feature, not a cost center.

## Floor rules (the banned list)

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact
- Explicit or sexual content involving minors — reported to authorities
- Explicit content outside of private, consensual adult conversation
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Spam, scams, solicitation, or selling
- Sharing anyone's private information (doxxing)
- Impersonation or fake identities
- Ban evasion

## The no-follow-up rule (locked)

A decline closes the conversation. **No follow-up messages, no re-pairing in
consecutive events, no workarounds.** Enforced in code, not just policy.

## Reporting

- Report from **any** chat — including during an event song.
- Reasons: harassment, explicit content, scam, minor-related, other.
- Every report is visible to a human. Automated filters catch the obvious;
  humans handle judgment calls and escalations.

## Blocking

- Block = invisible to each other, everywhere. Existing conversations close.
- Blocked users cannot see your profile in grids, search, or events.

## Bouncing (moderation ladder)

1. **Warning** — first offense, minor.
2. **Timeout** — temporary, proportionate.
3. **Permanent bounce** — serious or repeated offenses.
4. **Appeals** — anyone can appeal to a human via support. No secret bans, no
   permanent decisions without a human in the loop.

## Escalations

- Credible threats or minor-related content → law enforcement cooperation
  where required by law. (Draft — exact thresholds with counsel.)
